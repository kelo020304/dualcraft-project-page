OVERVIEW

Abstract

Generating articulated objects for robotic simulation from a single image requires both visual fidelity and correct kinematic relationships. However, models that appear plausible in a static view may exhibit detached parts, interpenetration, or missing interiors when their joints move. We present DualCraft, a multimodal agent framework that requires no additional model training and coordinates two complementary workspaces to generate executable articulated assets.

A visual editing workspace creates and refines part geometry, materials, and plausible interior structures to match the input image. An assembly programming workspace defines part assembly relationships and joint parameters to support correct assembly and motion. The agent iteratively refines appearance and articulation using rendered feedback from closed, intermediate, and open configurations, together with geometric and motion checks. A synchronization mechanism maintains explicit part and coordinate mappings between the workspaces and ensures that the exported asset matches the validated model.

We further introduce a retrievable template bank built from existing articulated assets, providing examples of part decomposition, assembly, and reusable geometry to guide the completion of structures that are difficult to infer from a single image. The evaluation protocol is designed to assess visual and geometric fidelity at unseen viewpoints and articulation states, alongside execution success across the motion range.

Visual authoring and executable articulation are refined together through multi-state feedback and explicit synchronization.
